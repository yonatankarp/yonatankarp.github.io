Summary

I'm a Staff Software Engineer. I've been writing software for 12 years: first C++ on vision processing chips at Inuitive, then backend payment systems at SumUp, and for the last three years risk and compliance at Billie.

At Billie I took two vendor integrations from an RFC to production, Ravelin for graph-based fraud detection and ComplyAdvantage for transaction monitoring. I also started three services from scratch and co-built the core of our Risk Orchestrator. A lot of my time went into the less visible work, like API contracts, dead-letter handling, monitoring, and being on call when something broke.

Outside work I contribute to open source (I added Spring Boot 4 and Jackson 3 support to openapi-generator) and write about Kotlin and backend engineering.

Staff+ Focus

  • Drove API-first backend design with Kotlin, Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, Flink, OpenAPI, and Backstage.
  • Turned operational risk into recovery paths, monitoring tools, migration plans, upstream fixes, and team-owned documentation.
