Summary

I'm a Staff Software Engineer. I've been writing software for 12 years: first C++ on vision processing chips at Inuitive, then backend services for SumUp's point-of-sale products, and for the last three years risk and compliance at Billie.

At Billie I joined the core architecture team and took two vendor integrations from an RFC to production, Ravelin for graph-based fraud detection and ComplyAdvantage for transaction monitoring. I also started three services from scratch and co-built the core of our Risk Orchestrator. A lot of my time went into the less visible work, like API contracts, dead-letter handling, monitoring, and being on call when something broke.

Staff+ Focus
